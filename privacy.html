Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Access

Through TikTok's official API with the account holder's explicit authorization, the App accesses: basic profile information (display name, avatar) and the ability to upload videos to the holder's own TikTok drafts. No data is accessed from any other account.

Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely on the account holder's own server. Tokens are never shared with third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties.

Data Deletion

The account holder may revoke the App's access at any time via TikTok's authorized-apps settings, which immediately invalidates all tokens.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the account holder directly.
